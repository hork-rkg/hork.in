HORK for Mac

How can we help?

Help with company accounts, inventory imports, HORK Cloud, billing, backups, barcodes and printers.

Email supportsupport@hork.in

Include your HORK version and a short description. Please never send a password, recovery code or complete business backup.

01

Accounts and access

Help signing in, activating an authorised device, understanding permissions or recovering owner access.

02

Cloud and AI

Check connection status in Settings → Cloud & Mobile. HORK AI also shows whether its selected service is connected.

03

Imports and records

For spreadsheet or photo imports, keep the original file and note which row, HORK code or filename caused the issue.

04

Printing and barcodes

Confirm the printer, paper and label dimensions in Settings → Hardware. Do not resize a barcode to force it onto different paper.

Before contacting support

1
Check system health

Open Settings → General, refresh the page, and run the system health check.

2
Record the version

Copy the version shown in Settings → General and mention whether the problem happens every time.

3
Protect your data

Create an encrypted HORK backup before reinstalling, restoring, resetting or moving to another Mac.

4
Describe what happened

Tell us the page, action, expected result and actual result. A screenshot is useful when it contains no private customer or business data.

Account and company deletion

An authorised user can request deletion of their cloud account in Settings → Security. A company owner can erase the company and its local content in Settings → Reset. If you cannot access HORK, email support from the address associated with the account. We may need to verify ownership before acting.
