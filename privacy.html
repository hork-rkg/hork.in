Privacy, explained clearly.

HORK is built to run a textile business—not to build an advertising profile. This policy explains what the Mac app and HORK Cloud handle, why they handle it, and the choices available to you.

Information HORK handles

The information depends on the features your company uses. It may include:

  • account identifiers, sign-in details, company roles and permissions;
  • company, supplier, customer and employee names and business contact details;
  • inventory, colours, quantities, prices, HSN codes, barcodes, racks and product photos;
  • sales, purchases, returns, payments, invoices and related business activity;
  • documents, imports, support messages, AI questions and user-selected attachments; and
  • technical information needed for security, licensing, synchronisation and troubleshooting.

Storage and security

HORK keeps an encrypted working copy of business records on the Mac. The local encryption key is protected by macOS Keychain. When HORK Cloud is enabled, authorised devices exchange company information over encrypted HTTPS connections so records can be backed up and synchronised.

No system can promise absolute security. HORK limits access by company account and role, and users should protect their Mac login, HORK password, recovery information and exported backups.

How information is used

Information is used to operate the features requested by your company: authentication, inventory and pricing, billing and returns, document generation, search and reporting, device synchronisation, optional AI assistance, customer support, security and misuse prevention.

Service providers and disclosure

HORK shares information only when needed to provide a feature your company uses—for example, infrastructure used to host HORK Cloud, email delivery, or an AI provider when a user submits an AI request. Providers receive only the information needed for that service. HORK may also disclose information when required by law or to protect users and the service.

HORK does not sell personal information.

Optional AI features

When a user sends a question or attachment through HORK AI, the request and relevant company context may pass through HORK Cloud to the selected AI provider, such as Anthropic or OpenAI, to produce the answer. Nothing is sent merely because the HORK AI page is open.

Users should not submit passwords, recovery codes or information that is not needed for the request.

Retention and deletion

Company information is kept while the company account is active and for limited operational backup, fraud-prevention and legal periods. An authorised user can request deletion of their cloud account from HORK under Settings → Security. A company owner can erase company content under Settings → Reset. Support can assist when the app cannot be accessed.

Deleting the app does not automatically delete information already synchronised to HORK Cloud. Exported files and backups remain wherever the user saved them.

Your choices

Depending on your role, you can export or back up company information, choose whether to use AI, remove records where the app permits, manage authorised users, and request account or company deletion. HORK can continue local work during some cloud interruptions and synchronise again when service returns.

Policy changes

If this policy changes materially, the effective date on this page will be updated. Important changes may also be communicated inside HORK or through the company account.
