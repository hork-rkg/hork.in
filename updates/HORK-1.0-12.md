# HORK 1.0 — Build 12

This combined update improves sign-in, invoicing, stock safety and HORK AI.

- Adds the new full-width HORK AI workspace with a visible Live Task Sandbox, clear work stages and approval before business data is changed.
- Adds **Remember this device** with a six-digit PIN for reopening HORK during the same Mac boot. A real Mac restart still requires the username and password.
- Fixes invoice search so choosing the same stock item increases its existing line instead of creating duplicates.
- Applies the available-stock limit consistently to search, barcode scanning, steppers and typed quantities.
- Removes zero-quantity stock from the sales item picker.
- Checks that customer mobile numbers contain exactly ten digits before lookup or customer creation.
- Repairs the complete OTA installer path used by the Intel MacBook Air.

This release is Apple-notarized and includes both Intel and Apple-silicon versions. Existing business data, licences and company settings are preserved.
