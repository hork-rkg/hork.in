# HORK 1.0 (Build 19)

This update refines day-to-day invoicing, pricing and people records.

- Sales and related invoice screens share a clearer, more consistent workflow, with improved item lookup and item details.
- Pricing has a focused ledger with its inspector on the right, clearer columns, selection-based changes, and print/export controls.
- Customer and supplier tables have more consistent column alignment and record actions.
- Startup and inventory-image work is organized to avoid repeating expensive work during common screen interactions.
- Universal Apple silicon and Intel Mac support. Requires macOS 12 or later.

Your existing business data is preserved during the update.
