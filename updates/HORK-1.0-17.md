# HORK 1.0 (Build 17)

This update improves pricing workflows and adds configurable print and export tools.

- Keeps pricing rules and the price preview in a right-side inspector beside the editable stock ledger.
- Adds field-selectable previews for pricing lists, inventory tables and barcode stock lists.
- Print or export selected rows and fields as PDF, CSV for Numbers or Excel, or an Excel workbook.
- Preserves the existing pricing save workflow; creating a report does not change stock or prices.
- Universal Apple silicon and Intel Mac support. Requires macOS 12 or later.

Your existing business data is preserved during the update.
