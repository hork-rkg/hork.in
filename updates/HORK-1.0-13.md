# HORK 1.0 — Build 13

Build 13 brings the newest workflow, reliability and assistant improvements together in one signed Mac update.

## What’s new

- Import suppliers from CSV, Excel, Word, selectable-text PDF, RTF or tab-delimited files.
- Download a supplier CSV template, choose the meaning of each column, review extracted records, and merge, skip or keep duplicates before saving.
- A wider HORK AI workspace with a visible task sandbox, suggested daily business work and clearer progress while the assistant is acting.
- “Remember this device” sign-in with an optional six-digit privacy PIN; a Mac restart still requires the account password.
- Safer sales entry: ten-digit Indian mobile-number validation, zero-stock items hidden from selection, duplicate item handling and stock-limit checks.
- Smoother inventory, pricing and barcode navigation, plus manual diagnostic reporting from Settings.

## Update reliability

This release is a complete universal update for Intel and Apple-silicon Macs. It is signed with HORK’s Developer ID, accepted by Apple notarization, and includes a repaired Sparkle updater signature chain.

Your local encrypted business file and cloud account are preserved during the update.
