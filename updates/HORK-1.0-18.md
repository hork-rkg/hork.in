# HORK 1.0 (Build 18)

This update makes invoice entry more responsive and gives Pricing a clearer, editable ledger.

- Phone-number lookup updates its customer panel without repainting the entire invoice and item grid on every keystroke.
- Pricing now has a searchable, filterable table with clear columns for stock identity, supplier, quantity, base cost, additions, landed cost, GST, proposed wholesale and MRP, and pricing status.
- Select individual rows or all visible rows, apply the pricing rule to the selection, review the result, then explicitly save.
- Keep the pricing inspector on the right with margin, cost, rounding and GST controls.
- Universal Apple silicon and Intel Mac support. Requires macOS 12 or later.

Your existing business data is preserved during the update.
