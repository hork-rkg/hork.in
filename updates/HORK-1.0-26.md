# HORK 1.0 (26)

Build 26 improves pricing, leads, and invoice history.

- The pricing tool stays visible alongside the stock list. Save, apply, or delete pricing presets, and select all visible stock in the pricing picker.
- Leads now have a clearer workflow for contact activity, follow-up scheduling, and order requests. An order request is saved in the lead; it does not file a sales invoice.
- Trade invoice lists use aligned filters and a proper row-and-column table without a supplier column or expansion arrows.
- Sales invoice history can correct a payment without changing the filed invoice's items, prices, date, or voucher number.
- Version displays include the build number, for example 1.0 (26).

Existing business records remain in place when the app updates.
