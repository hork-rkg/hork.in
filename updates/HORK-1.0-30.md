# HORK 1.0 (30)

Build 30 improves invoice handling, returns, and everyday navigation.

- Sales returns now show items purchased by the selected customer and reject items that do not belong to the original sale.
- Switching away from an unfinished invoice asks whether to hold or leave the draft, and voucher layouts fit smaller windows more consistently.
- Purchase entry and repeat payslip creation have been improved.
- The app-lock PIN field is ready for typing immediately; supplier rows no longer open automatically.
- People now includes Brokers, Leads dates show the year, and the financial-year selector filters lists and reports.
- Trade sections include Reports, and inventory print previews use the company heading.
- Update checking displays a result again when no newer build is available.

Existing business records remain in place when the app updates.
