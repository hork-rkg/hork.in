# HORK 1.0 — Build 22

Build 22 improves the daily trade and people workflows throughout HORK.

- Adds the redesigned Leads & Follow-ups workspace with a four-stage pipeline, weekly follow-up calendar, date filtering, search, lead inspector, and Print / Export.
- Improves invoice responsiveness and presentation across Sales, Purchase, Sales Return, and Goods Return.
- Improves invoice date, voucher, tax, type, item selection, manual-item, and selected-item layouts while preserving the existing voucher workflow.
- Allows Goods Returns to be filed directly against an active supplier, including mixed items from that supplier.
- Improves customer, supplier, employee, inventory, pricing, and barcode layouts, ordering, capitalization, editing, and export controls.
- Keeps HORK sign-in remembered across app updates and limits sign-in prompts to the intended Mac-startup security flow.
- Includes stability, performance, alignment, and accessibility fixes across the app.

Your existing business records remain on the Mac and are not removed by this update.
