# HORK 1.0 (31)

Build 31 improves purchase intake, reports, and invoice entry.

- Purchase entry starts with a new item instead of browsing stock already on hand. Enter an exact HORK code and confirm the matched supplier and item when restocking.
- Sales reports now show retail or wholesale type, supplier, and recorded payment method. Filter by date range, type, supplier, payment method, or repeat customers; totals follow the displayed rows and CSV export.
- Added invoice rows scroll inside the item table, keeping invoice details, totals, and actions in place. Extra blank rows can be removed, and the remove button is easier to see in Light mode.

Existing business records remain in place when the app updates.
