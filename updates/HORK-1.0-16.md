# HORK 1.0 (Build 16)

This update fixes the repeated hangs seen while entering customers on a sales invoice and improves the People directories.

- Fixes the spinning beachball and “not responding” state when typing, changing, or replacing a customer mobile number.
- Redesigns the customer-mobile section in Sales Invoice with clearer validation, customer-match feedback, and a cleaner layout.
- Restores customer names in the Customers table and prevents the first heading from collapsing to a single “C”.
- Adds aligned vertical column dividers to the Customers and Suppliers tables for a clearer grid.
- Improves HORK diagnostics so macOS hang and spin reports can be included when the user sends diagnostics to HORK.
- Universal Apple Silicon and Intel Mac support.

Your existing business data is preserved during the update.
