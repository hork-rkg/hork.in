# HORK 1.0 — Build 11

This update makes stock work smoother, especially on the Intel MacBook Air.

- Stops the barcode label preview from rebuilding continuously while its table scrolls.
- Caches barcode layout work until the selected item or label settings actually change.
- Prepares product images and shared inventory, pricing and barcode row data during the real post-sign-in progress screen.
- Shows a dedicated “Preparing stock pages” stage before opening the dashboard.
- Keeps support for 13-character custom barcodes on the 38 mm label.

After installation, HORK restarts automatically. Existing company data, licences and sign-in details are not removed.
