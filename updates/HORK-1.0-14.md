# HORK 1.0 (Build 14)

This update brings the latest invoicing, supplier import, inventory, AI workspace, accessibility, and performance improvements together in one release.

- Redesigned sales invoice list with proper rows and columns, date separators, advanced search and filters, financial-year and monthly totals, and selected invoice details.
- Preview or reprint old invoices, with configurable original, duplicate, and triplicate print copies.
- Manual invoice items, editable prices and GST treatment, bill discounts on screen and print, smarter customer names, and 10-digit mobile-number validation.
- Prevents duplicate item selection and quantities above available stock; zero-stock items are hidden from sales selection.
- Supplier import now supports CSV, Excel, Word, selectable-text PDF, RTF, and tab-delimited files with flexible heading recognition, field mapping, review, and templates.
- Improved HORK AI workspace and sandbox, business-data access, dashboard KPI refresh, and new-chat reset behaviour.
- Better keyboard navigation and shortcut guidance, clearer themes, MRP wording, custom HORK and supplier codes, and supplier-wise barcoding.
- Inventory lifecycle controls, smoother tables and barcode tools, diagnostics improvements, and more reliable in-app updates.
- Universal Apple Silicon and Intel Mac support.

Your existing business data is preserved during the update.
