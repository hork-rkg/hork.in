# HORK 1.0 — Build 25

Build 25 makes inventory work and invoice lists easier to scan and use.

- Inventory now shows total designs, pieces on hand, retail value, and wholesale value. The same figures can be selected for dashboard cards.
- Inventory tasks are grouped into batches instead of showing every design as a separate task.
- After prices are saved, the completed pricing list closes so the next batch is clear.
- Barcoding refreshes saved retail and wholesale logos, offers the retail company name as a label header, and puts header choices in one menu.
- The standard 38 × 55 mm label roll keeps its fixed measurements; gap and margin controls appear for measured rolls.
- All four Trade invoice lists use aligned search and filter controls, with a clearer calendar for the date range.
- The lead form keeps Status and follow-up controls aligned with the other fields.

Existing business records remain in place when the app updates.
