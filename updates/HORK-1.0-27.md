# HORK 1.0 (27)

Build 27 improves employee records, supplier payments, inventory export, and barcode performance.

- Employee records now support a picture and an internal payslip workflow with salary, bonuses, manual deductions, and advances.
- Supplier details include a separate account view to record payments and track outstanding purchase balances.
- Inventory print and PDF export use an A4 layout with a visible preview.
- Barcode list scrolling is smoother with larger stock lists.
- Leads forms have clearer spacing and section dividers.

Existing business records remain in place when the app updates.
